## Why these hooks fit Windy Hill specifically

- **Casual / walk-up volume.** A free, no-login-required-feeling scorecard + tee pick
  is exactly the value-add a high-turnover public course can offer without changing ops.
  Private-club "elevate your members" framing would be the wrong pitch here.
- **A complex, not just a course.** They have a range, batting cages, mini golf, and
  go-karts to cross-sell. The "deals/specials pushed to the phone mid-round" surface is
  built for exactly that — turn the golf round into a funnel for the rest of the property.
- **Family / F&B angle.** Turn coupons + saved offers + the snack-bar special feed map
  cleanly onto a family destination that already monetizes beyond green fees.
- **Local founder.** Same metro — lead with "I'll come show you on a phone," not a deck.

## Talking points if they reply / on the call

- **What they get (web dashboard):** push deals & specials with scheduling, run a live
  leaderboard for leagues/outings, configure per-hole/tee scorecard layout, see which
  offers players save (engagement signal — no redemption-tracking change required).
- **What their golfers get (free app):** live digital scorecard, smart tee pick by
  handicap, turn coupons, course specials feed, "My Saved Offers" they show at the
  counter, live leaderboard during events.
- **Pricing:** $200/month, flat, per facility. (Frame as the offer, not a number to
  justify — "$200/month, the player app is free, nothing to switch.")
- **Boundary line (say it plainly):** "This isn't a tee-time or POS replacement — it
  works alongside what you already run. It's the golfer's phone experience those systems
  don't cover."
- **Proof:** golfsync.io/course-manager-demo — offer to walk it live on a phone, in person.

> Caveat for Ryan: several player-facing surfaces (turn coupon, specials feed, saved
> offers, pace-of-play) are roadmap, not all shipped today. Course Manager v1 today is
> the web dashboard + per-hole/scorecard config + live iPhone preview + events. Pitch the
> vision, but if they want to start now, set expectations on what's live vs. coming —
> don't promise a ship date you haven't committed to.
